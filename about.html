About this site

BGE Benchmarks helps teachers search Curriculum for Excellence Experiences & Outcomes and their Benchmarks, without digging through PDFs.

This is an early rebuild of the original site: it now runs entirely in your browser with no database and no sign-in — your project list is saved only in this browser's local storage.

Benchmarks are extracted from the official Education Scotland benchmark documents for each curriculum area. Extraction is automated and mostly clean, but a small number of entries may have imperfect wording where the source PDF's layout was hard to parse — if you spot one, it won't affect the search, just double-check against the original document before relying on it.

Missing: Sciences, and a full pass over Gaelic (Learners) is still pending cleanup.

BGE Benchmarks — a free tool for Scottish teachers. Not affiliated with Education Scotland.
